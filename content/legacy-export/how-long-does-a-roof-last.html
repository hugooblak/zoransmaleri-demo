HOW LONG DOES A ROOF LAST?

 

It's one of the questions we hear most. The honest answer: it depends on the material, the weather and how well the roof was installed.

Typical lifespans

  • 3-tab asphalt shingles: 15 to 20 years
  • Architectural shingles: 25 to 30 years
  • Metal: 40 to 70 years
  • Cedar shakes: 20 to 30 years with care


What shortens a roof's life

Poor attic ventilation is the silent one. Heat and moisture build up under the roof and cook the shingles from below. Other causes are clogged gutters, overhanging branches, and a rushed install with too few nails.

"A good install with proper ventilation is worth more than the most expensive shingle."

How to make it last longer

  1. Clean gutters twice a year.
  2. Trim branches that touch the roof.
  3. Get a check after any big storm.
