What to do after a hail storm

Hail damage isn't always visible from the ground. Dents in gutters, downspouts and car roofs are a good clue that your roof was hit too.

1. Stay off the roof

Wet, damaged shingles are slippery. Take photos from the ground instead, including dents on gutters and siding.

2. Check inside

Look at ceilings and in the attic for damp spots. Put a bucket under any drip and photograph it.

3. Get a professional inspection

A roofer can spot bruised shingles and cracked seals that turn into leaks months later. Ask for a written report with photos.

4. Call your insurer

Most policies have a time limit for storm claims, often one year. Send them the report.

Watch out for storm chasers. Be careful with anyone who knocks on your door, asks for a large deposit, or offers to "cover your deductible". That can be insurance fraud.
