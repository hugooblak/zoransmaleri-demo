Roof repair or replacement: how to decide

Nobody wants to pay for a new roof if a repair will do. Here's how we help homeowners decide.

A repair usually makes sense when:

  • The damage is in one area, like a few blown-off shingles
  • The roof is less than 15 years old
  • The leak comes from flashing around a chimney or vent

Replacement usually makes sense when:

  • The roof is near the end of its life (20+ years for most asphalt)
  • You've had several leaks in different places
  • Repairs would cost more than about a third of a new roof
